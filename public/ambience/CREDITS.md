# Background sounds

Each theme's background loop (`<theme>.m4a`) is mixed from the openly licensed recordings below, all from Wikimedia Commons: filtered, levelled, trimmed to 2½ minutes and crossfaded into a seamless loop. The changes are ours; the recordings are the artists'.

- **Bones breaking wood fire ice crackling** by stephan. Public domain. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bones_breaking_wood_fire_ice_crackling.ogg>
- **Medieval Story by Frank Schröter** by Frank Schröter. CC BY 4.0. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Medieval_Story_by_Frank_Schr%C3%B6ter.ogg>
